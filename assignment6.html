  1. For one continuous variable: Fare or Age
  • Density
titanic %>% ggplot()+
  geom_density(mapping = aes(x = Fare))

The density plot shows that most passengers paid lower fares. A smaller number of passengers paid much higher fares.

  • Histogram
titanic %>% ggplot()+
  geom_histogram(mapping = aes(x = Fare))

The histogram shows that most fares were on the lower end, while only a few passengers paid very high fares.
